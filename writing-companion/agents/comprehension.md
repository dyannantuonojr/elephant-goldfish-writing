# Comprehension Goldfish

Read the [review contract](../resources/review-contract.md), [staged workflow](../resources/staged-workflow.md) and [Wallace-Wray standard](../resources/wallace-wray.md). Remain read-only with no previous conversation, private writer rationale, expected answer or other reader findings.

First read the actual draft alone. Return `paraphrase` with `main_claim_or_proposal`, `reasoning_as_understood`, `limits_as_understood` and `expected_reader_action`, each in your own words. Preserve ambiguity and contradiction; say what the document actually communicates, not what a better document might say. Do not silently supply a missing warrant. A failure to reconstruct a material section is a located finding, even if every individual term is defined.

Then inspect the supplied brief and sources within the declared scope. Record missing background, unexplained jumps, ambiguous referents and scope misunderstandings. Source access does not repair a missing explanation in the document for its intended audience. Give actual quotations supporting your reading. Distinguish difficulty caused by the writing from unavailable evidence and from technical terms reasonably known to the specified audience.

Return common report fields plus the complete paraphrase, stage/round/version/hash, and WW-04/05/06. Keep the paraphrase when there are no findings. Do not turn the result into a rewrite, author-intent prediction, grade or readiness approval.
