# Paper standard

The actual assignment, rubric, instructor guidance, required template and explicit user directions govern. Infer no universal scholarly source minimum, dissertation structure, research methodology or reference style.

## Before writing

Map each explicit requirement to a proposed section and needed support. Read supplied course/assignment material and the full model before asking for missing owner-specific facts. Treat an example paper as an example of depth/structure, not an authenticated source or the owner's personal experience. Preserve legitimate uncertainty in remembered classroom material.

## Argument and evidence

Identify the paper's question, answer/thesis, reasons, warrants, relevant counterevidence, limits and contribution. Connect source comparisons to this argument; avoid serial author summaries. Distinguish reported results, causal claims, correlations and the writer's application. Inspect populations, methods, dates, measures and transfer limits for the claims actually used. Sources known only by title, DOI, URL, search snippet or another agent's summary remain uninspected at that scope. Record accurate citations and reference parity; neither a valid identifier nor a company report earns peer-reviewed status by itself.

Use supplied actual source material or an already-authorized research route. Missing access is an evidence gap, not permission to invent support or add new provider accounts. A source with a notice/correction concern needs the actual notice checked; no blanket clearance claim follows from absence in an excerpt.

## Prose and closure

Each paragraph contributes a reason, evidence, qualification or implication; each section prepares a later step. Compare real adjacent paragraph passages to explain continuity. The conclusion synthesizes the answer, implications and boundaries established earlier rather than adding unsupported results. Maintain the owner's authentic voice and personal reflections. If required personal information is missing, identify it or ask once when needed; never manufacture autobiographical content.

## Review and delivery

Requirement coverage is a location/evidence map, not a grade. Distinguish ADDRESSED, PARTIAL, GAP and NOT_APPLICABLE with reasons. Maintain the requested length without removing necessary reasoning. References, appendices, notes and exhibits follow the actual submission rules. Use a truthful AI-use disclosure when required or requested. Document formatting, page count, links, accessibility and print appearance require inspection of the exact exported version; source review does not prove any of those checks.
